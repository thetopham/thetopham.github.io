Matrix · Latest work

A persistent world you can create with AI.

Matrix connects a Three.js/WebXR world to Codex, procedural creation, and Blender asset workflows. Use voice or text to create and change a scene, review the result, and keep refining it from desktop, VR, or AR.

v1.1 adds precise support-surface placement points, active-turn steering, and controls for moving a whole layout relative to a measured room. A separate PC-hosted AI Citizens prototype explores routines, needs, memory, and bounded interactions.

v1.1 released September 29, 2026. Local services require setup; v1.2 work is in progress.

Marinara · Interactive storytelling

A working path from roleplay to animation.

I implemented a MiniMax H3 / ComfyUI flow that turns roleplay narration into a storyboard, grounds characters with their sheets and personas, grounds locations with World Maps artwork, generates keyframes, and animates the resulting shots.

The tested flow produced a multi-shot clip with synchronized motion and audio. I also contributed World Maps, shared artwork, agent capability APIs, and recovery paths for persistent story state.

Manfred · Wearable AI

Daily audio, live voice, and visual context.

An Android/Flutter companion and Python services for wearable audio, transcription, search, and visual context. Manfred connects Omi audio and E09 smart glasses to a personal knowledge workflow.

Prototype: voice through the glasses works. Experimental photo capture and delivery still need reliability work.

Knowledge, briefs, and the machines behind the models.
