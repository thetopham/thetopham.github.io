Marinara · Stories into animation

Let’s watch the worlds we’re writing.

I’m working on a MiniMax H3 animation workflow. Each character’s reference sheet and written persona help keep them recognizable. World Maps adds pictures of locations so scenes can return to the same places—and aim to look like the fictional worlds fans know.

Our narration supplies the script; AI plans the scenes and starting images; ComfyUI brings the images and motion instructions together for video.
