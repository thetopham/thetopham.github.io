Systems View

Intro

Engagement-based feeds don’t just show content; they shape what feels urgent, normal, and real. Systems often reward strong emotion (especially anger), in-group identity alignment, and repeated exposure.

Background

  • Typical system goal: maximize engagement (watch time, clicks, comments, shares) to support ad revenue and data collection.
  • Key actors: platform teams, advertisers, creators/influencers, users, moderators/data workers, and regulators.
  • Ranking signals: predicted interaction, past behavior, network similarity, recency, and emotion/controversy features.
  • Bias-incentive coupling: negativity bias, affinity bias, and availability heuristic can all be amplified by engagement loops.

Impacts

  • Intended: personalization, speed, and relevance at scale.
  • Unintended: rage loops, polarization, viewpoint narrowing, and distorted risk perception.
  • Distributional: creators optimize for outrage; users pay attention and wellbeing costs; communities pay trust costs.
  • Accountability: opaque and shifting ranking logic makes responsibility hard to assign.

Reflection

This prototype makes the scoring function visible so the feed feels less like reality and more like a designed environment with explicit goals.
